How Dark Site Works

Dark Site uses CSS filters to intelligently invert the colors of web pages while preserving images and videos. This approach is similar to the popular Dark Reader extension.

  • Brightness: Adjusts the overall brightness of the page
  • Contrast: Controls the contrast between dark and light elements
  • Sepia: Adds a warm tone to reduce eye strain
  • Grayscale: Removes color saturation for a monochrome look

About Dark Site

Version: 1.0.0

Dark Site is a browser extension that makes all pages dark mode without breaking special content like images and videos.

This extension recreates the core functionality of Dark Reader, providing a comfortable dark browsing experience.
